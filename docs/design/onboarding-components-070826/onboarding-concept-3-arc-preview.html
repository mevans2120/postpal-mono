Why this composition

Lens: Asymmetric / Featured + Progressive. The Daybook already has a "What's ahead" chapter and a 1·3·5·10·20 day spine; this concept promotes that spine to the opening move. Showing the arc — day 1 marked as the hard peak, day 20 as the cycle — does the "believe her" and "benchmark against the range" work visually, before a single hard day arrives. Frame 3 literally collapses the arc into the real day-1 check-in, so the last thing she sees in setup is the first thing she'll use.

Strengths: best expectation-setting, orients her to the whole shape, fewest screens (three). Tradeoffs: denser than the calm budget prefers, and front-loading the full arc risks showing an anxious patient the scary peak before she's ready — the copy has to keep the arc reassuring, not ominous. The featured card and rail are new primitives to build.

Tokens used — the rail reuses --clay-soft / --clay-deep for the hard peak and --pine-soft / --pine for the cycle marker · --line for the spine & unreached dots · featured card on --clay-soft · day-1 preview reuses the exact face SVGs, --paper faces on --card · Petrona throughout, Albert Sans labels
