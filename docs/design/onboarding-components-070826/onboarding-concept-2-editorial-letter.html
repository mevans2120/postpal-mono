Why this composition

Lens: Editorial / Spacious + Typography-Forward. Every other surface in the Daybook already speaks in Carrie's voice through margin notes. This concept asks: what if the whole first contact is that voice? One continuous letter, Petrona throughout, with the single setup step (confirm) folded in as an inline moment rather than a gate. It's the warmest possible read on "made for her, prescribed by her doctor."

Strengths: the strongest trust and tone; setup never feels like a form; distinct from any generic health-app signup. Tradeoffs: more reading up front than the calm-budget rule usually allows (defensible here because pre-procedure she has the capacity that day-1 fog would steal), and the inline confirm control is a new interaction the check-in flow doesn't already have. Best if paired with a "skim" affordance for returning or low-literacy users.
